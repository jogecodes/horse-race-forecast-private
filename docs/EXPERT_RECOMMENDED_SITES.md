## TodoTurf: “Resultados” tip

According to the expert friend, selecting **Resultados** on TodoTurf lets you access all the races: “resultados para que te dé todas las carreras.”

The [results page](https://www.todoturf.net/resultados.php) was checked and presents **Año** and **Hipódromo** filters. Start there to inspect the available years and tracks. The claim that it includes all races is the friend's recommendation; complete historical coverage has not yet been audited or imported into this study.

## Blacktype name reference

An [issue on the magazine's own website](https://blacktypemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Black_Type_08_2019_06_13.pdf) identifies @BlackTypeTurf as its official account. This supports the likely identification above while preserving the name supplied by the friend.
